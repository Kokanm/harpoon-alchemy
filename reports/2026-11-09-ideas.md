# Brainstormed Game Ideas — Bubblewright (Roguelike Pang) Directions

- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Profile:** Solo dev + coding agents · $0–$100/mo · Unity 2D URP · Mobile + Desktop first, Web later
- **Seed:** *Bubblewright* (Roguelike Pang prototype — Steps 1–4 working in repo)
- **Private pool generated:** 14 ideas across 5 axes; 11 cut via hard gates and rubric scoring; top 3 retained below.

## Assumptions

1. **Code-first asset constraint:** Every idea must work with *Bubblewright*'s existing 480×270 (15 PPU) code-painted sprite pipeline (`PlaceholderArt.cs`, `PixelCanvas.cs`) and procedural synth audio (`Sfx.cs`), requiring zero external 3D art or hand-drawn level maps.
2. **Existing controls stay intact:** Wick remains floor-bound (no jump, 9 u/s run with 0.06 s accel / 0.04 s skid) with relative left-half thumb drag + right-half hold-to-fire (`TouchControlsView.cs`), which already fixes the #1 mobile complaint on *Pang Adventures* (fixed mini buttons causing missed inputs — `(Gamezebo; Apple App Store reviews, accessed Nov 2026)`).
3. **Dual-platform monetization fit:** Mobile uses hybrid-casual optional rewarded moments + single "Full Unlock / No-Ads + Cosmetics" IAP (or F2P Act 1 + $4.99–$9.99 full unlock like *BALL x PIT* and *Peglin* on mobile), while Desktop (Steam) uses a clean premium/demo model with the exact same core build.

### Idea 1: Harpoon Alchemy (Cable-Fusion Pang)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Core loop** | Drag left thumb to dodge bouncing spheres and hold right thumb to fire vertical cables → pop spheres into smaller children → draft and **fuse** two upgrade cards between stages to create screen-clearing cable reactions before the storm timer expires or 3 flame hearts go out. |
| **Why now** | Arcade ball-physics roguelites with mid-run **fusion/synergy** mechanics are surging across Steam and mobile: *BALL x PIT* launched Oct 2025 on Steam to Overwhelmingly Positive reviews (95% of 15,830 reviews) and expanded to iOS/Android in March 2026 with a free first act + $10 unlock `(Steam Store; Engadget, Mar 2026)`, while mobile Roguelike revenue grew +26.2% YoY to $371.9M and Hybrid-casual IAP grew +88% YoY `(AppMagic Monetization Report, 2025)`. |
| **Lifecycle stage** | **Rising** (est. 6–12 month window for compact arcade-fusion roguelites on mobile + Steam). |
| **Twist** | Instead of invisible stat boosts (`+12% run speed`), every upgrade alters **cable geometry or bubble split physics**, and combining two compatible cards on the between-stage Anvil creates a visual 2-card fusion: e.g., *Twin Cable* + *Anchor Tip* fuses into **Tesla Clothesline** (planting two vertical cables snaps a horizontal lightning wire between them that slices falling bubbles in half). |
| **Keep vs change** | **Keep:** Wick's floor movement, kinematic constant-apex bubble bounce math (`Bubble.cs`), 6-stage budget generator (`StageGenerator`), and storm timer. **Change:** Replace pure stat-multiplier upgrades in `Upgrade.All` with 10 elemental/geometric modifiers + a 15-pair Fusion Matrix that triggers visible chain reactions on screen. |
| **Automation lever** | Coding agents generate the 15 pairwise fusion behaviours in C# (`Harpoon.cs` + `Effects.cs`) using procedural pixel lines/particles (`PixelCanvas.cs`), and an automated headless EditMode simulation test fires synthetic harpoon patterns across 1,000 random seeds to verify every stage budget clears within 20–55 seconds without soft-locks or frame drops. |
| **Social hook** | **"Broken Fusion Build" 3-second clips + End-of-Run Build Card:** A muted 3-second clip shows two harpoon cables anchoring into the ceiling, a horizontal laser snapping between them, and an XL Trigloop splitting into 27 cascading firework pops in 1.5 seconds. Game Over / Clear screen renders a crisp 480×270 PNG card showing the seed, fused icons, and pop graph. |
| **Monetization** | **Mobile:** Rewarded ad to **Relight 1 Heart** on death (1× per run), **Reroll the 3 Upgrade Cards**, or **Preview a Fusion** before picking · Interstitial only on run end (never between the 6 fast stages if session < 3 min) · IAP: $3.99–$4.99 "Master Chandler Unlock" (removes forced ads, unlocks 3 starter launcher relics & seeded custom runs) · **Meta (v2):** Unlockable starter wicks/launchers via total pops. |
| **MVP scope** | Keep single rooftop arena; replace 4 stat upgrades with 6 combinable cable/pop upgrades + 6 pair fusions; add Anvil fusion UI badge on `DrawPick` cards; add 1-tap "Relight / Reroll" hook; target **10–14 days** for Android + iOS + Desktop build. |
| **Not in MVP** | Multi-world backgrounds, base-building meta (leave out *BALL x PIT*'s 70-building town), boss cutscenes, online leaderboards. |
| **Kill switch** | **Kill** if Android/itch prototype test shows D1 < 22% (global mobile P50 median) or daily playtime < 12 min `(GameAnalytics / InvestGame Mobile & PC Benchmarks, Jan 2026)`. **Iterate** at D1 22–34% / D7 7–14%. **Scale** if D1 ≥ 35% and D7 ≥ 15% with ≥ 22 min daily playtime `(Turbine Hybrid-Casual Playbook, Apr 2025; Gamigion Hybrid-Casual Overview, 2025)`. |

### Idea 2: Cage-Weaver Pang (Barrier & Bumper Arena)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Core loop** | Move left/right and fire harpoon cables that **stay anchored as solid bouncing walls** for 2.5 seconds → partition the arena to trap large bubbles inside narrow corridors with floating brass bumpers → rack up rapid ricochet combo damage before the cables snap or a bubble crushes Wick. |
| **Why now** | Physics-ricochet roguelikes (*Peglin*, 10,364 Very Positive Steam reviews and iOS/Android F2P-to-unlock port `(Steam Store; Pocket Gamer, accessed Nov 2026)`) and spatial-trap hybrid-casual puzzles dominate organic video engagement because viewers instantly understand "trapping a bouncing ball in a tight box." Meanwhile, *Pang Adventures* reviews on Steam and iOS complain that "only shooting straight up gives you too few defensive/tactical options" `(Steam Community Reviews, accessed Nov 2026)`. |
| **Lifecycle stage** | **Rising / Peaking** (physics-ricochet + spatial trapping is proven and evergreen on Steam & mobile; Pang-specific spatial trapping has zero direct competitors). |
| **Twist** | Harpoon cables don't pop big bubbles on first touch — Instead, `Anchor Tip` is the core verb: cables turn into **taut steel barriers** for 2.5 s. Bubbles bounce horizontally off your cables! Trap a bubble between a wall and your cable (or between two cables) with a floating **Brass Bumper** inside, and it ricochets back and forth at 4× speed, shaving off tiers and showering sparks until it bursts. |
| **Automation lever** | Extend `StageGenerator.Generate` to place 1–3 procedural **Brass Bumpers / Breakable Ledges** per stage using symmetry templates (`x = ±6`, `y = 6..9`), and run an automated kinematic trajectory check (`Bubble.Tick` math is deterministic!) to ensure no bumper layout traps a bubble above ceiling height. |
| **Social hook** | **"200 IQ Box-Trap" Shorts/TikToks:** A 3-second clip shows the player baiting two huge XL Lead bubbles into the right corner, slamming a harpoon cable down to lock them in a 3-unit cage with a bumper, and watching the combo counter hit `x32` in a loud chiptune crescendo. |
| **Monetization** | **Mobile:** Rewarded ad for **+1 Starting Bumper / Golden Barrier** on tough stages, **Continue with Shield**, or **Double End-of-Run Shards** · Failure-triggered low-priced starter pack ($1.99–$2.99, matching top hybrid-casual conversion drivers `(AppMagic Monetization Report, 2025)`) · **Desktop:** $4.99–$6.99 Steam release with free browser/Steam demo. |
| **MVP scope** | Add horizontal cable-deflection mode + durability counter to `Harpoon.cs`; add 2 procedural Arena Bumper types (Ricochet Bell + Spike Bumper) to `StageGenerator`; add Ricochet Combo multiplier to HUD; target **2 weeks** in Unity 2D. |
| **Not in MVP** | Complex polygon terrain, physics engine migration (keep custom deterministic math in `Bubble.cs`), multi-stage boss animations. |
| **Kill switch** | **Kill** if >40% of first-time players fail Stage 1 because they don't understand cable-wall trapping within 30 seconds, or if D1 < 22% `(InvestGame Benchmarks, Jan 2026)`. **Scale** if D1 ≥ 35%, D7 ≥ 15% `(Turbine Playbook, Apr 2025)` and organic clip CTR / completion beats baseline. |
| **Risks** | 1. **Trapping feels accidental rather than intentional on touch controls** → Mitigation: draw a faint 1 px dotted vertical aim guide above Wick's launcher and give cables a 0.4-unit magnetic snap when fired near a bumper. 2. **Trapped bubbles stall the player out** → Mitigation: cables pop whatever bubble touches them on their **3rd bounce** (3-strike tension snap). |
| **Scores** | Simplicity 4 · Content 5 · Virality 5 · Monetization 4 · Competition 4 · Timing 4 · Fit 5 = **31/35** |

## Recommendation: What to Build First and Why

**Build Idea 1 (*Harpoon Alchemy — Cable-Fusion Pang*) first, while borrowing the 1-day Seed Receipt from Idea 3.**

**Why:**
1. **Your movement, bounce math, and 6-stage loop are already done (`Game.cs`, `Run.cs`, `Bubble.cs`).** The single weakest part of the current prototype is the placeholder upgrade pool (`Upgrade.All` in `Run.cs:96-107`), where 6 of the 8 upgrades are invisible number tweaks (`+12% run speed`, `+30% harpoon speed`, `+0.75s invulnerability`). Invisible math upgrades don't show up in a muted 3-second TikTok/Shorts clip and don't create "one more run" synergy chase.
2. **Visual cable fusions turn *Bubblewright* into a true roguelite without needing new art.** Because harpoons, cables, and bubble splits are already drawn procedurally in code, adding 6 combinable upgrades with pairwise fusions (like **Tesla Clothesline** between two anchored cables, **Ricochet Barbs**, or **Volatile Split**) takes ~10–14 days in C# and gives you the exact synergy hook that propelled *BALL x PIT* (95% positive of 15.8k Steam reviews, plus March 2026 mobile launch).
3. **If you want to test your store/itch release pipeline this week first,** ship **Idea 3 (*Daily Wick Blitz*)** in 5 days as v0.1, then drop **Idea 1 (*Harpoon Alchemy*)** as the major v0.2 update.
